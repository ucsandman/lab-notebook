# 2026-09-16: Launch teardowns at scale

## What I tried
Reused the same delegation pattern for a second repo the same morning: launch-swipe-file, a collection of teardowns of great dev-tool launches, 5 to 8 to start, each following a fixed template (the launch, what they did, what worked, takeaways), every factual claim sourced with a verbatim link, each capped at about 40 lines.

## What worked
Six teardowns landed: Supabase (Launch Weeks as a format), Tailscale (technical blog post a month before launch), Linear (opinionated design as the headline), Cursor (bold positioning), Raycast (a documented launch-week playbook), Arc (invite-only waitlist as a launch engine). Weekly Friday cron to add 2-3 more. Verified afterward: 6 files present, commit edb16e9, clean tree.

## What failed
Nothing material. The second build went faster than the first because the first brief became a template.

## The lesson
A good brief is a reusable asset. The expensive part of delegation is writing the first complete brief; the second, third, and fourth are edits. When a pattern works twice, write down the pattern.
